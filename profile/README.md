## NOVA Privacy-First Ecosystem 

NOVA is a privacy-first, decentralized file-sharing primitive, empowering user-owned AI at scale with encrypted data persistence. It is used by talented developers, technology builders, to enhance online privacy across many industries and market segments. Take a look at the featured repositories and reach out if you want to add your project to NOVA's highlights.

### Health & Wellness / Personal Biometrics
Projects focus on secure storage and sharing of health, biometric, or personal monitoring data, leveraging NOVA for encryption and access control. Market data shows a growing demand in wearables, telemedicine, and personalized health (e.g., Apple Health competitors). NOVA for encryption and access control.

- [Proof of Pulse](https://github.com/NOVA-privacy-first/Proof-of-Pulse) — Biometric oracle for fitness/health apps (e.g., heart-rate proof-of-life).
- [NestHome](https://github.com/NOVA-privacy-first/NestHome) — Eldercare monitoring with encrypted event/images.
- [KeepAlive Protocol](https://github.com/NOVA-privacy-first/KeepAlive-Protocol) - Continuous biometric proof-of-life system, securely captures and stores encrypted vital signs.

### Finance & DeFi Tools
Projects emphasize secure financial data, trading strategies, or DeFi access with encrypted snapshots or transactions. Market shows massive DeFi/user-finance market (e.g., Zerion, DeFiLlama). Privacy for portfolios/strategies is a common pain point. NOVA enables secure sharing or audits.

- [PrivyFinance](https://github.com/NOVA-privacy-first/Privy-Finance) — Personal finance copilot with encrypted statements (personal data management).
- [BlindFold](https://github.com/NOVA-privacy-first/BlindFold) — Private crypto portfolio snapshots.
- [Cabo](https://github.com/NOVA-privacy-first/Cabo) - Encrypted trading strategy marketplace.
- [PTCID](https://github.com/NOVA-privacy-first/PTCIP) - SMS-based DeFi for unbanked.

### Social & Anonymous Communication (Community and User-Generated Content)
Projects build social features with anonymity, confessions, or community sharing, using NOVA for private posts/messages. Market shows revival of anonymous/social apps (e.g., Whisper, early Reddit). Virality from user-generated content. NOVA for private posts/messages.

- [unMasked](https://github.com/NOVA-privacy-first/UnMasked) - Anonymous dating and matchmaking based on private confessions.
- [Legion Social](https://github.com/NOVA-privacy-first/Legion-Social) - Builder community social platform.
- [Youtick](https://github.com/NOVA-privacy-first/YouTick) - Decentralized video platform (social sharing).
- [Crixen](https://github.com/NOVA-privacy-first/Crixen) - Social AI agent interactions.
